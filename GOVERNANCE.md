# Governance during the private prototype

Albert Tan Sin Hon owns the project and its commercial decisions. The development
workflow may make ordinary reviewed implementation improvements under his standing
authority. It must preserve private access, the rights position and commercial gates.

Piepowder and Symbolon have separate repositories and responsibilities. Separate
repositories do not create independent institutions, separate legal entities or
impartial adjudication. If both are operated commercially, ownership, appointment,
conflict and complaint arrangements need explicit review before a dispute service
is offered. A rules committee or neutral foundation has not been established.

Constructed examples are research fixtures. They must not be described as decided
cases, legal precedent, independent validation or accepted customer rulings. A real
precedent service would require permission, publication review, data minimisation,
conflict disclosure and governance for correction, reversal and withdrawal.

The founding report's commercial-code clauses remain proposals. The prototype's
policy file controls software behaviour only. It does not silently incorporate
liability caps, deemed acceptance, contra-drafter penalties or legal priority rules.

No case fee, customer price or institution partnership has been activated. The
commercial plan states experiments and evidence gates. Pricing and legal terms
require a concrete scoped offer before commitment. Buyer checks, current compliance
review, accepted terms, full actual available bank receipt and owner release apply
before customer-specific work or product access.
