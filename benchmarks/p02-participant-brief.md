# P02 first-use participant brief

Preparation version: `p02-session-kit/0.1`, 26 October 2026.
Session status: **NOT_PERFORMED**. This is an unissued fictional exercise.

## Your task

We are examining whether a person who did not implement Piepowder can use its
instructions to reconstruct a recorded acceptance result and explain its limits.
You are evaluating a private research prototype. The records, organisations and
events in this exercise are fictional. You are not deciding a real dispute or
authorising anyone to work, deliver a product or move money.

Participation must be voluntary and separately arranged through a permitted
route. You may decline a question, ask for help or stop. The facilitator must
explain the intended use and handling of your notes before starting. No identity
document, bank information, client data or real address dataset is needed.
Do not add such material to these files.

The facilitator gives you one card at a time. Complete the manual card before
seeing the software or its reports. For the assisted cards, use only the local
runtime and fictional input folders provided for this session. Do not browse
the benchmark labels or facilitator guide. Tell the facilitator if you have
already seen the examples, answer key or implementation, or if a person or AI
helps you. That disclosure is useful evidence, not a reason to hide difficulty.

Keep your original answers even if you later change your mind. Record uncertainty
in your own words. The facilitator records elapsed time, help and interruptions;
leave times blank if they were not observed. This is not a test of your speed.
You may answer in ordinary written notes or, with your consent, verbally for the
facilitator to transcribe. You do not need to edit JSON. Preserve your original
notes or permitted verbatim transcription separately from the structured form;
the facilitator must not improve or interpret your answers while recording them.

## Card M1: manual reconstruction

Complete this card without a Piepowder report. A calculator is permitted if you
record that you used one. Do not use an AI assistant for the initial response.

A fictional agreement contains this numerical rule:

- The supplied sample contains 1,000 records.
- The recorded number described as correct is 972; incorrect is 11; flagged as
  unresolvable is 17.
- The threshold is at least 98 percent.
- The permitted flagged share is at most 2 percent of all sampled records.
- The agreement does not specify whether flagged records count in the accuracy
  denominator.

The figures are supplied assertions. No independent sampling or ground-truth
validation is included in the card.

In the response form, record:

1. The numerical rule you can reconstruct and any choice the agreement leaves open.
2. The numerator, denominator and threshold comparison for each relevant reading.
3. The flagged share and its comparison with the cap.
4. Your conclusion, the evidence supporting it and what remains unresolved.
5. Any additional fact or decision you would require before relying on the result.

Save your initial response and tell the facilitator you have finished. Do not
revise it after seeing later cards; add a separate correction if necessary.

## Card S1: assisted reconstruction

Begin only after the facilitator releases the first assisted folder. It contains
a separate fictional case, an explicit numerical rule and supplied evidence.
Use the supplied local instructions to generate and inspect its analysis. Record
the exact commands you actually ran, any error and any help received. The
facilitator will provide the authorised local path and a new output filename.

Record the same five observations requested for M1. Also identify which files
and report fields support your conclusion, and explain how you could check that
you are referring to the same input bytes. Preserve the complete original report.

You may consult the supplied product instructions and local report during this
card. Ask for help when needed, but record it rather than treating assisted
completion as completion without help.

## Card S2: assisted reconstruction

Begin only after the facilitator releases the second assisted folder. Follow the
same local procedure with a fresh output file. Examine the supplied record before
drawing a conclusion. Record the rule, evidence available to you, any numerical
observation you can support, unresolved points and your proposed next step.

Explain whether the available record justifies the same kind of conclusion as
S1. If a calculation or conclusion is unavailable, state why. Do not fill missing
facts with an assumption or repair the fictional input to make the check pass.

## Final explanation in your own words

After saving S2, answer these questions without consulting the facilitator key:

1. What do the report's overall result and individual checks tell you?
2. What would a matching file digest establish, and what would it leave unverified?
3. How would you distinguish a numerical criterion that is not satisfied from
   evidence that cannot support a conclusion?
4. What, if anything, do these reports establish about identity, authority,
   actual geographic correctness, legal liability and permission to work or pay?
5. Could you repeat this exercise on a new permitted fictional case without help?
   Which step was confusing or depended on prior knowledge?

The facilitator will discuss the key only after saving your answers. A successful
exercise is evidence about this session. It does not establish general accuracy,
time savings, market demand or readiness for real customer inputs.
