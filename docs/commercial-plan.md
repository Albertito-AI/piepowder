# Commercial validation plan

The first customer hypothesis is a B2B automation or data-services operator that
repeatedly delivers structured work and loses time negotiating acceptance or
reconstructing the evidence. The recurring problem must be established before
building a broad dispute service. Existing RegNav prospects are not presumed
to be buyers for Piepowder.

## First product experiment

A bounded acceptance-and-evidence pilot could cover one recurring job type,
up to five expressly agreed criteria, ten permitted historical or synthetic
deliveries, a versioned specification, counterexamples and a reproducible review
dossier. One correction round would address failures against those written
deliverables. This is an offer design, not an issued offer or accepted order.

Pricing remains unapproved. USD500 is a possible internal fixed-pilot test point,
not an evidenced market price or a quote to any prospect. Before setting a price,
estimate support, preparation, customer acquisition, legal review and maintenance
costs, including the owner's actual time. The first order must have bounded
effort and positive contribution under an honestly recorded cost model.

## Evidence gates

| Stage | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- |
| Internal profile | One useful job type; at least 24 pre-labelled good, failed, underspecified and adversarial fixtures; retained failure results |
| Problem discovery | Up to three bounded operator interviews; two concrete recurring problems; one conditional willingness to consider a priced pilot |
| Funded pilot | Qualified buyer, accepted scope, actual bank receipt and authorised release; a comparable baseline on the agreed cases |
| Measured result | Review time, corrections and material missed issues measured against that baseline, with the small sample disclosed |
| Repeatability | A second independent order or paid renewal using substantially the same profile |

An initial decision target could be 30% lower median preparation/reconstruction
time with no missed material issue in the agreed test set. It is a hypothesis,
not a promised performance warranty. Failure to improve the process should lead
to revising or stopping the profile, rather than adding model panels to obscure
the absence of useful demand.

## Qualification and delivery controls

Prepayment activity is limited to proportionate prospect qualification and
discussion of the problem and standard scope. Do not analyse customer files,
run a customer-specific benchmark or deliver a free executable evaluation under
the label of discovery. Keep initial administration within the existing 30-minute
per-lead allowance and document a deeper review before expanding it.

Before customer work or access: identify the accountable buyer and authority;
confirm lawful and ethical intended use and input rights; complete the applicable
current compliance review; accept the same final scope, price, acceptance and
rights terms; independently verify the full agreed payment as posted and
available in the owner's verified bank account; and record the scoped release
approval and Start Notice. Processor balances and screenshots do not supply
the human bank attestation. Follow the actual agreed refund obligations and law.

No outreach, fee collection, live dispute or commercial commitment is authorised
by this plan. Any later sending action needs a verified destination and applicable
user authority. The current RegNav follow-up grant is specific to those conversations.

## Later business models

A maintained private review tool, repeatable profile licence or enterprise
integration may be worth testing after repeat demand appears. Per-case adjudication,
insurance data, public precedent and institutional referral remain later hypotheses
requiring separate consent, governance, privacy and economic evidence.
