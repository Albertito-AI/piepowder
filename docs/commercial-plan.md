# Commercial validation plan

The product hypothesis is a customer-run private package for making a supported
acceptance rule explicit and preserving the evidence needed to understand its
result. The first candidate workflow is a recurring geocoding or location-data
delivery, because the current engine implements one geocode-count profile.
Automation and data-services operators are a broader research audience; they
are not presumed to need this profile or to be qualified buyers. Existing RegNav
conversations do not establish demand for Piepowder.

The target is reusable software and records, with the customer able to operate
the supported workflow. Paid custom delivery is a bounded learning bridge.
Recurring value must be observed before a recurring licence is offered. A
working private prototype does not establish commercial release readiness.

## First product experiment

Use at most two bounded, funded learning pilots on the already-supported profile.
A proposed scope is one recurring job type and up to ten expressly permitted
historical or synthetic deliveries, with a versioned criterion, evidence manifest,
counterexamples and reproducible review dossiers. Agree the input limits,
deliverables, acceptance method and correction allowance before commitment.
Include a customer-run exercise after authorised release, and record onboarding
and support effort. Reuse substantially the same package in the second pilot.

The profile checks supplied counts; it does not geocode addresses, verify sampling
or establish factual correctness. A documented criterion is not automatically
executable. New profiles require a separately approved, bounded scope and an
evidenced reusable need. Do not expand a pilot into a general semantic evaluator,
workflow assurance service or legal review.

The earlier unapproved price placeholder is superseded. Pricing, financial goals,
cost assumptions and account research belong in the private operational records.
This repository contains no active quote or financial forecast. A concrete offer
must account for development, acquisition, administration, delivery and support.

## Evidence gates

| Stage | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- |
| Problem discovery | Bounded discussions of a recent recurring problem, the existing process, responsible buyer and reason to consider the stated scope; no customer-specific analysis or access |
| Internal profile and first use | P02: at least 24 pre-labelled good, failed, underspecified and adversarial fictional fixtures; retained failures; independent human first-use and reconstruction review |
| Permitted operation | Minimum P03 boundary: accountable authorised operator, permitted and minimised inputs, documented local trust and data-handling limits, reviewed intake threat model and explicit absence of legal/payment authority |
| Funded pilot | Qualified buyer, accepted scope and rights, current compliance review, no owner HOLD, actual available bank receipt, scoped owner release and a comparable baseline |
| Measured result | Preparation/reconstruction time, corrections, material missed issues, customer-run completion and support effort; sample and method limitations disclosed |
| Reusable package | Another independently paying customer using substantially the same package/profile, useful repeated operation and support that fits the stated product scope |
| Recurring offer | Observed recurring use and willingness to pay for continued access, with defined licence, update, support and expiry terms; separately authorised activation |

An initial decision target could be 30% lower median preparation/reconstruction
time with no missed material issue in the agreed test set. It is a hypothesis,
not a promised performance warranty or a claim that a small sample establishes
general reliability. Compare with the customer's existing checklist, spreadsheet
or test process. Record adverse results and assistance as well as successes.

P02, independent human first-use review and the minimum P03 boundary precede
customer operation. P01 remains open and unimplemented; a Symbolon adapter is
conditional on an evidenced need for its pinned exports. No adapter is required
for ordinary problem discovery or an otherwise ready existing-profile pilot.
The [launch plan](launch-plan.md) sets the rollout and review decisions.

## Qualification and delivery controls

Prepayment activity is limited to proportionate prospect qualification and
discussion of the problem and standard scope. Do not analyse customer files,
run a customer-specific benchmark or deliver a free executable evaluation under
the label of discovery. Keep initial administration within the existing 30-minute
per-lead allowance and document a deeper review before expanding it.

Before customer work or access: identify the accountable buyer and authority;
confirm lawful and ethical intended use and input rights; complete the applicable
current documented compliance review and confirm no owner HOLD; accept the same
final scope, price, acceptance and rights terms; obtain human verification that
the full gross invoice payment reconciles through only documented, agreed
seller-borne fees and permitted FX to posted, available proceeds in the owner's
verified bank account; and record scoped owner release and the Start Notice. Processor balances
and screenshots do not supply the human bank attestation. Bank receipt alone
does not authorise work, evaluation access or licence activation. Material changes
to buyer, payer, intended use, recipient or scope reset the relevant gates.
Follow the actual agreed refund obligations and law.

This plan does not itself authorise outreach, publication, spending, a live dispute,
commercial commitment or licence activation. Sending requires a verified suitable
destination and applicable user authority. Authority for another project's
conversations does not automatically extend to Piepowder.

## Private operational ownership

The project owner retains commercial and release decisions. The designated private
nonfinancial commercial register tracks prospect stages, permissions, agreement
and compliance references, communications, payment-attestation references and next
actions. Underlying identity, agreement and bank evidence remains in its designated
private case record.
The existing financial owner retains the financial records. Customer data,
identities, correspondence, bank evidence, pricing and account research stay
outside Git. The engineering record keeps only permitted, non-identifying findings,
supported capability changes and reproducible fictional validation.

## Later expansion

Additional profiles and integrations require observed repeatable demand. Per-case
adjudication, insurance data, public precedent and institutional referral remain
separate hypotheses with additional consent, governance, privacy and operating
requirements. They are not capabilities of the current product.
