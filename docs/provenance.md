# Source and build provenance

The owner supplied `Piepowder_Agent_Judiciary_Report.docx` as the founding
research report and requested that the project be brought to life. The original
is retained unchanged in the private project archive, outside Git.


Version 0.1.0a1 is an AI-assisted implementation and review prepared on
25 October 2026. It selects the report's prevention-first approach for a
bounded offline experiment. Research corrections and implementation decisions
are new working records; they do not silently amend the founding report.

The source report's proposals for public governance, licences, autonomous
adjudication, custody and commercial terms have not been activated. No claim
is made that every report assertion has been independently established.
The legal review is issue-spotting against cited primary materials, not an
operative agreement or jurisdiction-specific clearance.

All committed scenarios are fictional. No customer correspondence, personal
identity documents, bank records or original prospect files are included.
The geocode counts illustrate competing denominator treatments; they do not
represent a measured service or customer delivery.

The project owner retains the rights position recorded in `RIGHTS.md`.
Symbolon remains a separate repository with its own existing rights and
licence position. No Symbolon source code has been incorporated into this build.

Reproducibility means the same supported bytes and algorithm reproduce the
same record. It does not prove consent, identity, actual time, underlying facts
or the correctness of a legal conclusion.
