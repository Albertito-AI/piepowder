# Private product launch plan

Planning revision: 25 October 2026. This file defines evidence gates; it does not
record a launched service, activated licence, customer result or sending authority.
Use the [commercial plan](commercial-plan.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md), current
[validation record](validation.md) and [security boundary](../SECURITY.md) together.

## Product and claims

The intended product is a reusable customer-run package for one supported
acceptance workflow. The current private alpha checks recorded geocode counts
and preserves the criterion, evidence bindings, numerical readings and unresolved
questions. Its outputs are technical observations for review.

Do not claim factual geocoding accuracy, verified sampling, authenticated identity,
per-event authority, trusted time, general prose interpretation or legal effect.
Work and payment authorisations remain false. The procedural docket is a local
simulation. Software tests and fictional cases do not establish independent use,
customer demand or production readiness.

## Rollout gates

| Gate | Required evidence | Result permitted by the gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | A recent recurring acceptance or evidence-reconstruction problem, the existing method and a responsible prospective buyer | Decide whether further bounded qualification is useful; no customer-specific analysis or product access |
| Readiness | P02 labelled benchmark; independent human first-use/reconstruction record; minimum P03 operator/input boundary; unresolved material defects addressed or explicitly excluded | Owner may assess a concrete supported pilot for release; this does not itself authorise release |
| Commercial release | Agreed scope/rights, current compliance review, no owner HOLD, full actual available bank receipt confirmed by a human, and explicit scoped owner release | The agreed work and named access only |
| Learning | First of at most two funded pilots; fixed supported profile and sample; baseline and customer-run exercise; corrections and support recorded | Decide what reusable product improvement, if any, is justified |
| Repeatability | Another independently paying customer using substantially the same package/profile; useful repeated operation without bespoke seller delivery | Assess a private licence with explicit users, permitted outputs, updates, support and expiry terms |
| Recurring use | Demonstrated ongoing use and willingness to pay, with support within the agreed scope | Consider a recurring offer through a separate concrete commercial decision |
| Public release | P07 rights, security, validation and publication gates | Only the expressly authorised public material or release |

P01 remains open and unimplemented. Build its Symbolon adapter only when an
evidenced workflow needs a pinned export. Existing-profile discovery and pilots
do not require an internal ecosystem integration. New profiles require their own
bounded approval and a repeatable need.

## Experiment and decision record

Set the question and expected observation before each experiment. Record the
supported profile/version, permitted sample, existing process, outcome measures,
operator assistance, failures and what would disprove the proposed benefit.
Keep case records and identifying evidence in their designated private case
records; the nonfinancial operational register tracks references and next actions.

For discovery, start with a small number of bounded conversations. Ask about a
recent delivery and its actual review effort; do not infer demand from compliments,
general interest or an organisation's service page. Customer files are not needed
for this discussion. Stop when the existing qualification allowance is exhausted
unless a further bounded step is authorised under the commercial workflow.

For the labelled benchmark, fix expected outcomes before running checks and retain
adverse, missing, disputed and ambiguous cases. Independent human first use must
test whether someone outside the implementation work can follow the instructions,
reconstruct a result and explain its limitations. Record coaching and failed steps;
an agent review or an internal test run is not a substitute for that evidence.

For a released pilot, compare reconstruction/preparation effort, corrections,
material missed issues and support against the agreed baseline. Disclose the small
sample and selection method. A favourable result is evidence for the tested case,
not a general accuracy, dispute-prevention or time-saving guarantee.

Continue when the recurring problem, useful repeated operation and sustainable
support are evidenced. Narrow or change the profile when a specific observed gap
explains failure. Stop or defer when no useful recurring problem appears, the
same package cannot be reused, or delivery requires open-ended custom effort.
Do not conceal a failed experiment by adding unrelated features.

## Tracking and review ownership

The project owner retains release and commercial decisions. The designated private
commercial register tracks permissions, qualification, scope, compliance reference,
communications, next action and due date. Financial records retain their existing
owner. Prices, targets, account research, identities, customer files and bank evidence
do not enter Git. A material change resets the relevant qualification/release gates.

Within the existing authorised routines, review active commitments and blockers,
hold a weekly evidence/effort review and make a periodic continue/change/stop
decision. Each review should select at most one justified next improvement and
record its acceptance check. Respect the shared active-work limit; do not create
a parallel development scheduler.

Recurring automation may surface evidence, stale stages and decisions due within
its authorised sources. A reminder or status transition cannot authorise outreach,
publication, spending, client work, payment movement or licence activation.
Record engineering outcomes in the improvement log and current validation record;
retain private commercial details only in the designated private records.
